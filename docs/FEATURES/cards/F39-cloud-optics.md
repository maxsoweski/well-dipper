# Feature Card — F39 Cloud Optics

Domain: Optical · Lab status: 🔴 not-built · Build-seq phase: 4c

## 1. Description (WHAT)

F39 Cloud Optics (domain: Optical, family F-optical). Physical chain: P26 optical/atmospheric scattering — colored optical effects produced when sunlight backscatters off **uniform-droplet clouds**, riding on the F31 cloud deck. The **core deliverable is the backscatter GLORY**: concentric, discrete colour rings centered on the **antisolar point** — the point on the cloud deck directly opposite the Sun from the camera, where the line of sight looks back along the sun line. This is the same geometry as the colored rings around an airplane's (or a mountaineer's, the "Brocken spectre") shadow on clouds — diffraction of light grazing minute, *monodisperse* (uniformly-sized) cloud droplets, reflected once and re-emitted back toward the source. Real-body example: **Venus** — ESA's Venus Express orbiter directly *imaged* a glory in the Venus cloud tops in 2014 (observed 24 July 2011), the first time a glory was fully imaged on another planet; the droplet monodispersity it implied let scientists size the cloud particles at ~1.2 µm. WD carriers: the F31-cloud-bearing terrestrial worlds — **terrestrial, ocean, venus** (Venus is the canonical/brightest carrier — the Venus-Express phenomenon).

**Stylization directive — the WORST envelope-fit in the catalog (tracker:110-130).** A real glory is sub-degree concentric *continuous spectral* rings — the single highest-frequency colour detail in the entire feature set, exactly what the 6-level posterize + Bayer dither is purpose-built to destroy. F39 does NOT fight that: it leans INTO discreteness. The build renders a **2–3 banded ring read as DELIBERATE posterized colour steps, not a failed smooth gradient** (tracker:128-130). The bands are hard-quantized `theta/uGloryRadius` steps mapped to a constant warm-outer→cool-inner palette (reddish outer ring → green → blue-violet inner), a flat palette with NO composition lookup (mirrors airglow's flat green). Two further deliberate non-physical stylizations: (a) the ring radius `uGloryRadius` is **inflated far past the sub-degree real angle** so the rings actually *read* at planet distance (a real glory would be sub-pixel at d15); (b) the pipeline does NOT model uniform-droplet microphysics (droplet-size monodispersity) — that input is simply missing, and the build invents/stylizes past it. These are stylization calls, NOT accuracy claims.

F39's identity in one line: *the only antisolar concentric colored-ring feature — 2–3 hard posterized colour bands on the LIT cloud deck, centered where the camera looks back along the sun line.*

## 2. Current shader approach (HOW, as-built)

**ABSENT — build from scratch.** No glory, rainbow, cloud-optics term, uniform, registry key, or GUI folder exists in `world-engine-lab.html` or `src/` (confirmed: no glory/rainbow/cloudOptics identifier anywhere, per tracker:90-91). §6.5 below is the build contract.

TARGET approach (what to build): an additive emissive term `cloudOpticsC` on the **★ EMISSIVE-bypass channel** — the post-quantizer family (the `gl_FragColor` `min(...)` sum at ~L4063, where `auroraC`/`airglowC`/`bioC`/`cityC` already live) that SKIPS the 6-level posterizer, so the glory's discrete bands are never *double*-quantized by the Bayer dither. It must NOT fold into the posterized `cloudC` (which IS posterized at ~L3636) — the glory rides *beside* the deck, not inside it. The term is the product of three things: a **glory ring** (discrete colour bands quantized from the backscatter angle `theta = acos(dot(V, uLightDir))`, V = view-to-camera, stepped against `uGloryRadius` into 2–3 hard hue steps), a **dayside gate** (`smoothstep(0.0, 0.1, diff)`, `diff = max(dot(shadeN, uLightDir), 0.0)` — the OPPOSITE of airglow's night mask; a glory is a lit-cloud-top phenomenon), and a **cloud-deck gate** (`uCloudCoverage > 0.0`, the F31 master gate). Intensity is `uCloudOpticsIntensity`, gated so OFF or a cloudless world produces exactly `vec3(0.0)`. The bypass channel is what lets the discrete bands read as clean colour steps rather than as a posterized banding artifact (the same property that makes aurora/airglow work there).

## 4. Math / modeling notes (HOW, from the field)

Physics: a glory is a **backscattering** phenomenon — light is diffracted around minute, uniformly-sized cloud droplets, reflected once internally, and re-emitted almost exactly back toward the Sun. It therefore appears only at the **antisolar point**: the direction directly opposite the Sun from the observer, i.e. where the observer's own shadow falls. Brightness peaks where the view-to-camera direction aligns with the sun direction.

The central math (the glory ring):
- View-to-camera direction: `vec3 V = normalize(cameraPosition - vPos);` (the live form at ~L3302).
- Object-space light direction: `uLightDir` (the substellar uniform the frag reads, decl ~L263 / vertex decl ~L183).
- Backscatter alignment: `dot(V, uLightDir)` → **1.0** at exact backscatter (antisolar point), falling off as the view swings away.
- Angle from exact backscatter: `float theta = acos(clamp(dot(V, uLightDir), -1.0, 1.0));` — 0 at the centre, growing outward.
- Discrete rings: quantize `theta / uGloryRadius` into 2–3 hard steps (e.g. `floor(theta/uGloryRadius * 3.0)`), each step mapped to a constant palette colour (warm reddish outer → green → blue-violet inner), zero beyond `uGloryRadius`. This is the **deliberate posterization** (tracker:128-130): the band edges are crisp colour steps, NOT a smooth interpolation.

Gates (both required, both already live in the shader):
- **Dayside gate** — `float diff = max(dot(shadeN, uLightDir), 0.0); float lit = smoothstep(0.0, 0.1, diff);` (~L3298). A glory is a LIT cloud-top effect — this is the exact OPPOSITE of airglow's night mask. The glory lives where the cloud tops face the Sun (and the camera looks back through that lit deck along the sun line).
- **Cloud-deck gate** — `uCloudCoverage > 0.0` (the F31 family master gate; `cloudC` built ~L3633, `uCloudCoverage` decl ~L223, per-frame writer ~L7922). No deck ⇒ no droplets ⇒ no glory.

Why the radius is stylized-large: a real glory subtends well under a degree; at planet distance d15/d4 that is sub-pixel — invisible. The build DELIBERATELY inflates `uGloryRadius` so the 2–3 bands span enough screen angle to read as concentric rings. This is a known non-physical stylization, documented as such (§1). Likewise the pipeline carries NO droplet-size (monodispersity) input — the real driver of glory ring spacing — so the ring geometry is invented/stylized rather than derived from cloud microphysics.

Envelope survival (the standalone challenge, the WORST in the catalog, tracker:110-130): a physically-literal glory is sub-degree continuous spectral rings — the highest-frequency colour detail in the feature set, which the 6-level posterize + restrained palette crushes into 2–3 hard colour steps reading as a banding *artifact*. The stylization directive flips that liability into the design: render the 2–3 hard steps ON PURPOSE, as deliberate posterized colour bands, and keep them on the emissive-bypass channel so the Bayer dither never touches them (no double-quantization). The same property that makes aurora/airglow read as clean self-luminous forms is what lets the glory bands read as intentional steps rather than crushed gradient.

Drivers/gate: P26 optical/atmospheric scattering; gate on the F31 cloud deck (`dependsOn:{features:['clouds']}`, `isolationKit:['clouds']`) AND on the lit hemisphere. Constant palette — NO composition lookup (mirrors airglow). No seeded spatial variation (the ring is geometric — it follows sun + camera, not a per-seed pattern).
