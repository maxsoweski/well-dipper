# Feature Card — F38 Airglow

Domain: Optical · Lab status: 🔴 not-built · Build-seq phase: 4c

## 1. Description (WHAT)

F38 Airglow (domain: Optical, family F-optical). Physical chain: P24 photochemistry — the *non-magnetic* half of the night-limb glow story (F37 aurora is P24's D13-magnetic half; F38 is the photochemical half). Atoms and molecules in the upper atmosphere, excited by daytime solar UV, re-emit light at night as they shed that energy (chemiluminescence + recombination), producing a faint continuous self-luminous shell that encases the *whole* planet. From orbit, looking edge-on into the upper atmosphere, this reads as a **thin emissive band hugging the night-side limb at ALL latitudes** — not a polar feature. Characteristic color is **airglow green** (atomic-oxygen forbidden line OI 557.7 nm, peaking ~95–100 km). Variability signature: steady, latitude-uniform, faint; intensity scales with atmosphere density and zeroes on airless worlds. Real-body example: Earth airglow (the green limb band in ISS astronaut photos). WD types: terrestrial, ocean, venus — **plus Mars and Titan** (the model's narrow list was widened at build review: both have *real* airglow — ESA's Trace Gas Orbiter directly detected Mars's green 557.7 nm nightglow in 2020, the first visible airglow seen at another planet; Titan glows in its thick N₂ atmosphere — and both are already in the F38 archetype union, so the gate lights them honestly; see §7). Gated on atmosphere presence/composition D4/D6, NOT on the magnetic field. Inventory status: `[subtle]` — flagged subtle in the catalog; the build directive (tracker:123) is to BEAT the 6-level envelope by stylizing toward a DEFINED discrete band, not a smooth fade.

**Distinctness contract (the whole reason F38 is a separate feature).** Three already-built `[current]` features paint the same night-limb region; F38 must read as DISTINCT from all three (tracker:103-108):
- **vs F37 aurorae** — aurora is a *polar ring/oval* colored by atmosphere composition and gated on the magnetic field (D13). F38 is *uniform at all latitudes*, always airglow-green, and gated on atmosphere alone (no field). Aurora has curtain striations + flicker; F38 is smooth and steady. On a world with both, F38 is the continuous green band the polar ovals sit on top of.
- **vs F34 limb / atmosphere rim glow** — F34 is a *broad* atmospheric-scattering halo (fresnel rim, blue line, thick haze) spanning a wide band of the limb. F38 is a *thin* line right at the limb edge, narrower than F34's halo, and emissive-green rather than scattering-blue.
- **vs F33 nightside glow** — F33 is a *general fill* of the dark hemisphere (broad nightside luminance). F38 is pinned *at the limb edge only*, not a hemisphere fill — it traces the grazing-angle ring, leaving the interior of the night disk dark.

F38's identity in one line: *thin + uniform-all-latitudes + airglow-green + sitting right at the limb edge on the dark hemisphere.*

## 2. Current shader approach (HOW, as-built)

**ABSENT — build from scratch.** No airglow term, uniform, registry key, or GUI folder exists in `planet-lod-lab.html` or `src/` (confirmed: no airglow identifier anywhere, per tracker:90-91). §6.5 below is the build contract.

TARGET approach (what to build): an additive emissive band on the **★ EMISSIVE-bypass channel** — the post-quantizer family (lines ~3995–4031 in `planet-lod-lab.html`) that aurora/sunglint/city-lights/bio already live on, the one that SKIPS the 6-level posterizer so a glow term is never dithered into a banding artifact. The band is the product of two masks on that channel: a **night mask** (the live aurora form `1.0 - smoothstep(-0.1, 0.1, diff)` — edge0 < edge1; the reversed `smoothstep(0.1,-0.1,…)` card-prose form is spec-undefined GLSL and must NOT be copied) and a **limb mask** (a fresnel/grazing term `pow(1.0 - abs(dot(N, viewDir)), k)` that is ~0 across the face and rises to a thin bright ring exactly at the limb). Per the tracker's stylization directive (tracker:127-130), the band edge is made **DEFINED and higher-contrast** via a sharp `smoothstep` on the limb term — a discrete band, NOT a smooth low-contrast fade. The smooth fade is precisely what the posterize+Bayer-dither envelope destroys; surviving the envelope is the standalone challenge (tracker:99-102). Color is the hard airglow-green constant ~`vec3(0.35, 0.95, 0.55)` (a `uAirglowColor` uniform). Intensity is `uAirglowIntensity`, driven by atmosphere density (zero on airless worlds), with a `> 0.0` guard so an off/airless world produces exactly `vec3(0.0)`.

## 4. Math / modeling notes (HOW, from the field)

Physics: airglow is photochemical emission, distinct from aurora's particle precipitation. The dominant visible line is OI 557.7 nm (atomic-oxygen forbidden green), emitted from a layer ~95–100 km altitude; sodium ~85–90 km gives a yellow-orange fringe below it and OH gives red — but the green oxygen line is the canonical "airglow" read and the one WD models. It is *continuous and global* — a thin luminous shell over the whole planet, ~one-billionth the brightness of the Sun, so it is only visible over the dark hemisphere where nothing brighter washes it out. Critically it is NOT magnetically organized: no oval, no poles, no composition-color lookup — just a uniform green band wherever the limb is dark.

Why a *limb* band and not a hemisphere fill: airglow is optically thin, so from orbit you only accumulate enough column emission to see it where the line of sight runs *tangent* to the shell — i.e. at the grazing-angle limb. Looking straight down through the face you pass through too little of the layer to register; looking edge-on at the limb you integrate a long slant path through the emitting shell. That is exactly a fresnel/grazing term: `limb = 1.0 - abs(dot(N, viewDir))` is ~0 face-on and →1 at the limb, raised to a power `k` to thin it into a ring. This is the mechanism that distinguishes F38 (at-limb ring) from F33 (nightside fill) at the math level — same dark hemisphere, different angular mask.

Envelope survival (the standalone challenge, tracker:99-102): a physically-literal airglow is a *smooth low-contrast* gradient, and the 6-level posterize + Bayer dither crushes a smooth low-contrast gradient into either one flat band (indistinguishable from the body edge) or nothing. The stylization directive (tracker:127-130) is therefore to stylize toward a **defined, discrete, higher-contrast band**: make the limb mask a SHARP `smoothstep` (narrow edge0→edge1 window on the grazing term) so the band has crisp inner/outer edges, and keep it on the emissive-bypass channel so the posterizer never touches it. The bypass channel is what lets a thin bright green line read as a clean self-luminous arc instead of a posterized artifact — the same property that makes aurora/sunglint/city-lights work there.

Drivers/gate: P24 photochemistry; gate on atmosphere presence/composition (D4/D6) and terrestrial/ocean/venus archetypes — NOT the magnetic field (that is aurora's D13 gate). Intensity scales with atmosphere density (`atmoFactor`), so a thick atmosphere reads brighter and an airless world reads exactly zero. No seeded spatial variation (v1 cut) — the band is deliberately uniform, which is also half its distinctness contract vs the structured aurora.

## 5. Isolation recipe (:9223)

Once built, isolate via the registry solo path (F38 WILL have a key in `planet-archetypes.js` FEATURES, unlike aurora). On the :9223 second Chrome (chrome-devtools MCP, per well-dipper-testing-reference.md): (1) open `planet-lod-lab.html`; (2) `window._lab.setPreset('Rocky (Earthlike)')` — atmosphere retained → `uAirglowIntensity` derives nonzero via `atmoFactor`; (3) solo it: `window._lab.solo('airglow')` (registry-driven) OR manually silence competing night-limb terms — `uniforms.uAuroraIntensity.value = 0` (kill the polar ovals), `uniforms.uLimbStrength.value = 0` (kill F34 halo), `uniforms.uEmissive.value = 0`, `uniforms.uCloudCoverage.value = 0`; (4) crank for inspection: `window._lab.uniforms.uAirglowIntensity.value = 1.0`; (5) it is night-masked — orbit the camera to the dark hemisphere (`state.yaw`/`state.pitch`) and look at the terminator limb; the band should appear at the limb at ALL latitudes, not just the poles; (6) distances: `window._lab.state.distance = 15` (whole-limb ring read), then `4` for the band-thickness/edge-crispness read. Gate checks: `setPreset('Frozen (airless)')`, `'Lava (hot airless)'`, `'Europa (icy moon)')` → airglow vanishes (no atmosphere → `atmoFactor` 0 → intensity 0). Distinctness check: enable aurora AND airglow on Rocky simultaneously — confirm the green band is uniform all-latitudes while the aurora ovals are pinned at the poles ON TOP of it. Best presets: 'Rocky (Earthlike)' and 'Ocean (temperate)'; 'Venus (sulfuric shroud)' is the thick-atmosphere carrier (brightest band).
