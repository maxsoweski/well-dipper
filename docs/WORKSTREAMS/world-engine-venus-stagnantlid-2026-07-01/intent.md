# world-engine-venus-stagnantlid-2026-07-01 — intent

**Increment #4b** of the world-engine history program (GROUND track). A new three-free relief
writer for the **stagnant-lid silicate** regime — Venus — the sibling of the shipped `plates.js`
and `shellRelief.js`. Serves the SCREENSAVER heart via the program's driving outcome: *the count of
genuinely distinct, history-coherent worlds visible per minute* — here, giving Venus its own real
surface instead of the latitude-band smear every un-written world falls back to.

## Why we care

Max's framing this session: *"We are in the ground workstream here. We need to keep programmatically
working through the procgen models that give us all the varieties of ground seen in the galaxy's many
different worlds until they're all functional."* Venus is one of those varieties — a **stagnant-lid
silicate** world, the single most-recognizable non-Earth rocky planet — and today it is **not
functional**: it's a `NAMED_BODY` with `archetype=null, locked:false`, so it falls through every relief
gate to the despun `sin²(lat)` fallback and reads as horizontal bands. This increment makes Venus
functional: it gives it a history-data relief field organized by **one seeded mantle-plume field** (the
real Beta–Atla–Themis causal logic), so the screensaver shows the instantly-Venus silhouette —
**tessera crustal plateaus, corona rings, and a young basaltic-plains sea** — not a smear. It is a
BROADEN move (one archetype from "same as 8 others" to "its own world-type"), a pure sibling writer
that never touches the validated plate, ice-shell, or volcanic paths.

## Success criteria (Max's language)

*(Drafted from the committed BUILD-READY mechanism doc + Max's program framing above, for greenlight —
not invented cold. Max confirms or redirects before any code is written.)*

- **Venus stops reading as latitude bands.** It shows the Venus silhouette — tessera plateaus, corona
  rings, and young basaltic plains — organized by mantle plumes, not by latitude.
- **The elevation ordering a real Venus has:** the old tessera highlands sit highest, the basaltic
  plains sit in the middle, and the rifts / active-corona trenches sit lowest.
- **Coronae read as rings, not decals or dots:** some active (a raised dome with an outer trench and
  rise), some inactive (a raised rim around a sunken interior) — placed by the plume field and
  clustered like the real BAT region, not sprinkled at random.
- **Different seeds give visibly different Venus worlds** (different plume + corona layouts), each one
  reproducible from its seed.
- **Nothing else changes.** Earth-like, ocean, icy/despun, and volcanic worlds stay byte-identical —
  only Venus gains a surface. Drop Venus's one routing line and it must fall back exactly as before
  (single-coverage fragility — tested both ways).
- **(Holistic — Max's gate alone)** Venus reads as a distinct, recognizably-*Venus* world from the
  user's view.

## Deliberate non-goals (carried from the mechanism doc)

- **NOT Mars** — a different stagnant-lid-*rocky* shape (Tharsis rise + cratered highlands + hemispheric
  dichotomy); needs its own research pass and writer. 4b is Venus-only.
- **NOT the 4a active-volcanic branch** (Io shields / lava plains / magma seas) — separate, already shipped.
- **NOT a simulated resurfacing time-history** — active/inactive coronae are a static morphology SELECTOR,
  the resurfacing-age field is a datum (generative, not simulative).
- **Seed-only** — driver-response (the `tune`/`drivers` seam) is deferred to a future 4b-MULTIPLY, mirroring
  how #4-MULTIPLY followed #4a.
- **`resurfAge` stays in diagnostics only** for 4b (no `carrier.resurfAge` channel yet — deferred to the E12
  palette increment to avoid the `maturity`/`baseLevel`/`standing` channel collision).
- **Lab-only** — no game `Planet.js` port (that is increment #9).
