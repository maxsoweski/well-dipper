# Form references — Max, 2026-07-28

Two images given at UAT on `1056f30`, after the re-spec (boxy screens on arms, two vertical
strips, no nose, cabin light off). They are **vibe references, not blueprints** — Max's words
were "basically the vibe I'm going for" and "bottom can be simpler, more like this."

## Reference 1 — `max-vibe-reference-2026-07-28.jpg`

Max: *"this is basically the vibe I'm going for but with less instrumentation at the bottom"*

What it establishes:
- **Screens are boxy units on arms**, mounted high and angled inward toward the pilot — this is
  the direct visual source for "on arms coming from outside the player's POV." The arms attach to
  the canopy structure at the edges and reach in; the screen bodies have real depth and a visible
  bezel, exactly the 1″/2″ brief.
- **Chunky angular canopy struts** at the left and right of the view, crossing, converging toward
  the top — the structure that reveals the canopy's 3D form.
- **Visible cabling / conduit** running across the canopy interior. A cassette-futurism detail
  worth noting for a later increment; not increment-1 geometry.
- **Green phosphor displays** — already the project's palette (`scan-phosphor-style.md`:
  ink `#EDE8DE` on `#000`; this reference is greener than Well Dipper's warm off-white).
- ❌ **What Max explicitly does NOT want from it:** the dense lower instrument cluster — roughly
  six screens plus keypads and dials across the bottom.

## Reference 2 — No Man's Sky cockpit (pasted inline, **not on disk**)

Max: *"bottom can be simpler, more like this"*

Recorded in words because the image was pasted into chat rather than supplied as a file:
- **Faceted angular canopy frame** with clear straight-line breaks — mullions dividing the opening
  into flat panes. Reads as folded metal, not moulded plastic. Matches "angular, can be very simple."
- **Two prominent struts** running from low-centre outward and upward to the top corners, forming a
  shallow V. Their bends are what sell the canopy's protruding shape. This is the closest visual
  match to Max's "two strips running vertically and giving you a sense of the canopy's protruding
  shape" — note they read as **structural members set in from the extreme edges**, not as ribs laid
  over the centre of the glass.
- **Four boxy screens low and outboard**, two per side, angled inward toward the pilot, each with a
  bright bezel outline and real body depth.
- **A modest central console** at bottom centre — one holographic element, nothing dense.
- Large open sky through the canopy; the frame reads as thin relative to the opening.

## What this changes vs. what was briefed to the rebuild

The rebuild in flight was briefed with the ribs as **Spitfire/P-51-style ribs running over the
forward bulge**. Both references instead show **structural members inset from the view edges,
bending as they rise** — closer to A-pillars with a kink than to canopy ribs. The bend, not the
curvature, is what conveys protrusion. Correct this in the next iteration.

Also: "remove the nose for now" is **not** the same as "the bottom is empty." Reference 2 keeps a
modest lower canopy sill under the screens. The nose (exterior hull seen over the bonnet) is gone;
a simple lower frame edge is still wanted.
