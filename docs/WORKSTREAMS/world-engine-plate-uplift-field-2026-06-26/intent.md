# world-engine-plate-uplift-field-2026-06-26 — intent

**Campaign:** World-Engine production-L1 port — Option C (one-pass plate placement), increment 1: a placed plate/uplift field feeding the existing erosion. **Renderer:** LAB only (`world-engine-lab.html`); game `Planet.js` production-renderer port deferred/OUT.

This is the **first increment of Option C** — the one-pass plate-placement model the 2026-06-25 generative-architecture assessment named (`docs/FEATURES/world-engine-genarch-assessment-2026-06-25/ASSESSMENT.md` §6 Option C, §9 FLAG, §10 Q2) and held as "the sequenced next step after A lands." Option A (the coarse baked-relief render port) is increment-1-built and verified (`docs/WORKSTREAMS/world-engine-baked-relief-render-2026-06-25/`, `verdict.json`), so by the assessment's own sequence (A → C, `ASSESSMENT.md:255`) this is the correct next unit. The assessment's adversarial §11 is blunt about why this increment, not more of A, moves the north star: *"the CONTENT the BUILD engine writes is the unsolved problem — both the slice and the base step produce latitude-banded, amplitude-keyed, same-class-identical layout BY THEIR OWN ADMISSION… The E6 plate-scope question (Option C) is closer to the crux than A's plumbing"* (`ASSESSMENT.md:328-332`). This increment takes on that crux: place plate-shaped structure as **data** for Earth-like bodies, so there is something coherent for the renderer to read and for rivers to drain.

The mechanism is the one-pass pipeline the project's own research already specified — **centroids → spherical-Voronoi partition → Euler-pole motion → boundary-stress classification → write an uplift field U (high at convergent boundaries, rift/negative at divergent, low in cratonic interiors) → spread to interiors** (`ASSESSMENT.md:211`; `research-plate-tectonics.md:50-62, 105-113, 240-245`) — feeding WD's existing, UAT-passed bounded erosion. The whole thing is built and UAT'd **in the lab**; the production-renderer port is a separate later increment (lab≠game by charter).

## Why we care (Max's words)

The world engine is a **story engine** — a planet should READ AS, and actually BE, *"its own billions-of-years history."* Max's north star this session, in his words:

> *"READS as coherent is important, but I actually want it to be COHERENT. That way, we can create the kind of variety of planets and moons that will make a galactic exploration screensaver compelling."*

And the question this increment exists to answer in the affirmative — does the system *"carve rivers where the underlying World Engine procgen tells us rivers SHOULD BE based on the planet's history across all variables our generative engine outputs?"* Right now it does not: the on-screen relief is the un-carved, latitude-only E6 tectonic grain (`tectonic.js:19` `stressAtLat` reads only sin²(lat) → bands, not plates), and a correct flow-router over an incoherent substrate produces incoherent rivers. Coherence is bounded by the weakest input, and today both inputs (a latitude-only height substrate and uniform rainfall) are historyless. Placing real plate structure is what lets the rivers run where the history says they should.

The model is **generative, not simulative** — in Max's framing, *"what HAPPENED over 3 billion years given system/formation variables,"* NOT *"what happens over 3 billion years."* The history is authored as a determined end-state implied by initial conditions, and rendered once. The one-pass placement honors the spine §0 lock *"place once, don't time-step billions of years"*: every reference system the project surveyed does this in one shot, and *"that one-pass model is fully compatible with 'place plausible structure once per body'"* (`research-plate-tectonics.md:306-323`).

This serves the planet-and-moon **variety** the screensaver needs — which is exactly why the plate path is an **addition by regime, not a replacement** (`ASSESSMENT.md:216`): Earth-like terrestrial bodies get plates; icy/tidally-locked/despun bodies keep the despun-shell physics that is *the right model for them*.

## The bar this increment is judged against (Max's words)

The bar is **coherent, not just reads-as-coherent**: the relief must come from a generated field whose structure is **plate-shaped** — first-generation continents with uplift concentrated at classified convergent boundaries, rift/low ground at divergent boundaries, and quiet low cratonic interiors — not from noise that merely happens to look bumpy. The failure mode this whole increment exists to avoid is *"place uplift-as-noise + erode = eroded noise"*: a deterministic noise field would pass a weak "it's finite and stable" test and still ship incoherence. So the bar is that U **correlates with plate-boundary geometry** — that the structure IS somewhere meaningful, which Option A's plumbing by itself does not achieve. (This is why AC2 makes a noise control and a latitude control *required to fail* — the bar is encoded as a test, not a hope.)

Holistically, the bar Max alone renders (UAT): looking at an Earth-like body as a whole, *does it read as coherent first-generation continents* — distinct landmasses with ranges at the convergent margins and drainage cut into that same generated relief — rather than latitude bands or scattered noise.

## Success criteria (Max's bar, in the established model's terms)

> The direct quotes above are Max's; the mechanism detail in these bullets (plates/Voronoi/boundary-class/U) is the team's established SOTA model — the means to his bar, stated so the criteria are testable.

- For an **Earth-like terrestrial** body, the low/mid-frequency relief comes from a **plate/uplift field (data)** — continents are placed as structure (plates partition the sphere, boundaries are classified, U is written high at convergent boundaries, negative at divergent, low in cratonic interiors), the same every time for a given body/seed. Not re-invented noise, not latitude bands. The plate field is the **sole** low/mid-frequency source for the Earth-like body (it replaces the latitude-band writer rather than adding on top of it — additive would re-introduce the banding this increment removes).
- **Drainage follows that placed relief** — the rivers route and carve on the same field the surface structure lives in, so water runs down the real generated relief. This is the literal sense of *"carve rivers where the World Engine procgen tells us rivers SHOULD BE."*
- The placement is **generative and render-once** — a one-pass plate placement plus WD's existing **bounded gen-time erosion relaxation**, no time-stepping of billions of years. (Bounded passes ≠ time-stepping: the precedent is the UAT-passed Stage-2 carve, which already runs a bounded relaxation and renders once — `relief-e9-hydrology.js:104` runE9 5-pass; `tectonic.js:131` jacobiSmoothSphere 10-pass.)
- **Built C-ready, no rework debt:** U is a **swappable interface** — the one-pass placement writes U today; a future Tier-C plate-*motion* stepping pass writes the SAME carrier.height, and everything downstream reads the carrier field (+ the routed-graph `accum`), never the plate objects. The erosion consumes U through that interface, not inlined.
- **Branch by regime — don't globally swap E6:** the plate path is **gated to Earth-like terrestrial bodies**. Icy / tidally-locked / despun / stagnant-lid / volatile / impact bodies keep the existing despun/zonal E6 output **byte-unchanged** (same calls, same args, same seed). Variety preserved; nothing that worked before regresses.
- **No north-star debt for the next increment:** the erosion's discharge input is **parameterized now** — the hardcoded uniform rainfall (`planet-lod-rivers.js:489` `accum = 1`) becomes an *accepted* precip-weight array (omitted ⇒ byte-identical to today), and the carve accepts an external base-level/sea-level field (omitted ⇒ today's scalar sea-level byte-identical) — so the precipitation/climate increment drops in later with **zero rework**.
- **Variety from seed:** different seeds produce genuinely distinct macro-layouts (different plate counts / boundary geometry / continent arrangement), not one shape rescaled — what makes a galaxy of bodies compelling rather than one planet recolored.

## Explicitly NOT in this increment (named follow-ons, not abandoned)

- **The precipitation / climate field itself** — the NEXT increment. This increment only *parameterizes* the discharge and base-level inputs (replaces the hardcoded `accum = 1`, accepts an external sea-level field) so the precip field drops in with zero rework; it does not generate or apply a precip field.
- **Driver-response tuning** (making plate count / vigor / age respond to the formation drivers — the P3 'Orogeny' set `['tidalHeating','massGravity','habitability']`, and/or `surfaceHistory`/`age` for terrane character). This increment seeds placement from the **macroSeed** for seed-variety; wiring the driver vector into plate parameters is a separable follow-on, and `route()` does not yet thread the driver vector (`planet-lod-rivers.js:1112` — "grainDrivers default to the neutral E6 bundle until WS1 wires through").
- **The game `Planet.js` production-renderer port** (Option A's render path onto the game sphere). `Planet.js` + `MaterialBodyShader.js` carry zero river/carve/relief-bake code today; "onto the sphere" here means the LAB sphere. The production port is a separate later increment — lab≠game by charter; isolated-harness-first.
- **Tier-C plate-*motion* stepping** (Cortial-2019-style 2-Myr steps adding accreted terranes, suture lines, worn old belts, irregular continents). This increment is **Tier B now, built C-ready**: it ships first-generation continents via one-pass placement and *structures* U so a future stepping pass can layer in without rework. The stepping pass is a future increment.
- **Province-as-referent rewiring** (making the in-shader province partition — `initProvinces` `planet-lod-rivers.js:229` / `gProvince`, keyed off `PROVINCE_GROUPS` in `planet-feature-associations.js:17` — derive from the plate/uplift field instead of the abstract noise partition). This increment *structures* U so provinces COULD later key off it (convergent-boundary uplift ↔ `tectonic-highlands`, cratonic interior ↔ `old-plains`), but does not rewire the province source. Considered and deferred.
- **Non-Earth-like regimes** (icy / tidally-locked / despun / stagnant-lid / volatile / impact bodies). They keep the existing despun/zonal E6 model unchanged here; each becomes its own future increment. This increment touches only the Earth-like terrestrial path.
