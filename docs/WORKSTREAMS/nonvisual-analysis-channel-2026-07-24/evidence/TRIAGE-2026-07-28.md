## ⭐ THE HEADLINE — finding [1] is CONFIRMED, and it is Max's lead, inverted

> *"The instrument samples the synth FBM body, not the baked relief the planet actually renders
> at the live default."*

**Verdict: REAL, high, unrefuted.** Independently re-established by the refuter, which also closed
the one reachability hole the adjudicator left open.

The mechanism, end to end:

- The instrument's shader program is `HEIGHT_FRAG = HEIGHT_GLSL + ROUTER_MAIN`
  (`planet-lod-rivers.js:358`), whose base term is the **unconditional**
  `vec4 hd = fbmd(vPos, uOctaves, fwBase);` at `planet-lod-rivers.js:323` — no strength branch,
  no cube fetch. It always reads 100% synth.
- The **lab planet** branches at `world-engine-lab.html:381` (`if (uReliefBakeStrength > 0.0)`) and
  at `:385` multiplies the synth term by `(1.0 - strength)`.
- At the **shipped default** — `reliefBakeStrength = 1.0` (`:2546`), `planetRadiusEarth = 1.0`
  (`:2258`), `bakeReliefCrossover(1) = 1` exactly (`planet-lod-lab-core.js:129-130`) — the planet's
  synth weight is **exactly 0** while the instrument reads **100% synth**.
- The baked path is genuinely live, not a dead branch: `world-engine-lab.html:6430` runs
  `ensureNetworkRouted('initial')` at boot, which bakes the height cube.

**The instrument and the renderer were measuring different bodies.**

**This is Max's carrier-off suspicion, with the polarity flipped.** His filed lead was that the
legacy shader systems may not be wired *into* the procgen model. What the code actually shows is
the legacy synth wired **into the measuring path** — the instrument never saw the carrier at any
radius. Same fault line, opposite direction. It is the only one of the 38 that is downstream of
this fault; the refuters struck decorative attributions elsewhere.

### What it costs us in banked results

The adjudicator flagged `changesBankedResult: true`; the refuter sharpened it, and the sharpened
version is the one to act on:

1. **No banked *value* moves — a banked *claim* must be requalified.**
   `RADIUS-CENSUS.md`'s row `Substrate / relief | ✅ WIRED | MEASURED | ∝ R^0.458` is not supported.
   The measured exponent is fully explained by the **display lever** (`uDispDomainScale = sVis`);
   the World Engine relief writer (`carrier` / `writeBodyRelief`) was never in the sampled field.
   The census's own Tectonics row (`substrate.js`, `sphereField.js`: no radius, no gravity) already
   sits in tension with it.
2. **A standing banked number was measured on a half-rendered body — and the adjudicator missed this.**
   The refuter caught that only the **R = 0.5 and R = 1** rows of `AC-SAMPLE-live.md` are withdrawn
   (by the `spectralExcessPeak` correction block). The **R = 2 row — form size 35.1 km, 0.158°,
   excess ratio 1.86 — is NOT withdrawn**; it anchors the surviving "35 → 51 → 71 km, ratios 1.46
   and 1.39, ~√2" reading. At R = 2, `sVis = √2`, `|log₂ sVis| = 0.5`, crossover = 0.5 → the planet
   rendered a **50/50 carrier/synth** body while the instrument read 100% synth.
3. **Not established:** the adjudicator's guess that the carrier body's exponent "would be nearer 0
   than 0.5" is **unmeasured speculation**. Treat as `NEEDS_MEASUREMENT`, not as a result.

### The integrity control that could not have caught it — finding [17]'s refuter

`AC-CURVE-live.md` calls the `radiusEarth` control row (`1.0000 ± 0.0000, r² = 1.000`) *"proof
inside every sweep that the driver actually moved."* It is not. `fieldProbe` reads
`sample.radiusEarth` back from **the same `state.planetRadiusEarth` that `setPoint` just wrote**
(`world-engine-lab.html:6971`). The control proves the state variable moved and that `fitPowerLaw`
works — **it cannot prove `applyDrivers` propagated radius into the field.**

That is a **tautological control, and it is exactly the property finding [1] exploits**: the driver
moved, the display lever moved with it, and nothing in the control could have noticed that the
relief writer never did. Neither the 2026-07-25 reviewer nor the adjudicator named this; it came
out of the adversarial pass.

## Four defects found *during* triage that are not among the 38

1. **`fieldSampler.js:191-192` computes `tan(mean angle)`, not the mean gradient** (Jensen —
   `tan(mean(atan(r))) ≠ mean(r)`). So `AC-CURVE-live.md`'s surviving `gradient (mean) | 9.4%`
   noise-floor row is the CV of a nonlinear re-map. It is a monotone transform, so the CV remains
   usable and **no banked number is invalidated** — but `meanGradientUnitsPerHorizontal` is the
   wrong name for what it computes, which is the same class of mislabelling as [13] itself.
2. **`descriptors.js:388-389`** — legacy branch with zero live callers, kept alive solely by
   `descriptors.test.js:238`. Dead-code surface in the module whose premise is that unexercised
   paths are where silent drift hides.
3. **`stats.js:244-247`** — the `sd=0 / n≥2` Infinity branch is deliberate but has zero test coverage.
4. **[11]'s implied fix does not work.** Replacing `|mean| ≤ sd` with `|mean| ≤ z·sem` only *raises*
   the hard ceiling (45 → 89), it does not remove it. The fix must say what replaces the ceiling.
