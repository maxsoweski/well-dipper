## The guarantee, and how it is verified here

Since increment 3e (ac5-decision.md Addendum 3), a system's name resolves as:

> **1. Named-systems catalog (shipped, finite).** A build-time-authored table
> (`src/generation/data/namedSystemsCatalog.js`) of settled bare words
> ("Veshara") + greek notables ("Alpha Vozara 4821"), keyed by the injective
> position locator. Uniqueness / blocklist / key-collision / round-trip are all
> checked AT BUILD TIME. This is the FIFTH real-object mechanism.
>
> **2. Procgen (two classes).** For every other position, a **pure, injective**
> function of canonical galactic position (no registry, no persistence): the
> position quantizes to a fixed lattice at `Q = 4e-6 kpc`; the three lattice
> coords pack into one mixed-radix locator `L` (~70 bits); and each of the two
> classes — survey designation (base-36 coordinate field) and multi-part fantasy
> (word + code) — embeds `L` injectively. Distinct positions → distinct `L` →
> distinct names.
>
> **Shape exclusivity.** The two procgen shapes (survey has " J" + latitude sign;
> multipart has "-") are structurally disjoint from the two catalog shapes (bare
> `^[A-Z][a-z]+$`, greek `^Word Word \d+$`), so a procgen name can never equal a
> catalog name. Uniqueness holds end to end.

The named-via columns below drive the **exact production call-site RNG chains**
(`warp-star-<idx>` sky-click, `warp-nav-<seed>` NavComputer, `feat-<seed>`
feature route). The generator **ignores** those seeds — which is why every path
agrees and revisits are stable. This tool asserts that agreement on every sample.
