# Cockpit interior — Max's direction for the rebuild (collected verbatim)

The interior gets rebuilt after the exterior settles. Existing components can be rescaled and repositioned.

- 2026-10-02: "The interior is going to have to be rebuilt anyway. The basic components that were created are fine.
  They can be scaled up or down if necessary, and mostly I was going to ask for a bunch of changes to how they are
  oriented and where they are placed, at what angle, and so on within the cockpit."
- 2026-10-03: "the waist-level dashboard elements should be on a plane like a desk surface in front of the player's
  torso. It should be at an angle relative to the player's position not straight on out from the wall."
  (CC reading: one angled desk plane in front of the torso, tilted toward the seated pilot; the waist-level screens and
  controls sit on it, instead of each projecting straight out from the wall.)
