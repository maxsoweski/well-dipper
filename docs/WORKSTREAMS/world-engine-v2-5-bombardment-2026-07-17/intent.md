# world-engine-v2-5-bombardment-2026-07-17 — intent

## Why we care

The program's dead-lid worlds are blank canvases: when Frozen rerouted to the despun
writer, Max read it as "bumpy" — generic terrain with no story. Bombardment is the
increment that answers that note with real cratered history: a crater population
*scheduled by the body's conditions* (gravity, age), written as a **persistent host
that later epochs edit** — which is also the cleanest de-risk of the #6 epoch editor
the whole history program is driving toward. And it finally gives impact-airless
worlds (Moon/Mercury) a real world-type instead of no preset at all.

## Success criteria (Max's rulings, 2026-07-17 interview)

- Craters live in a **new separate crater layer** the renderer blends at draw time
  (the shelfDepth pattern) — the 75 golden byte-fingerprints stay green. ("Sounds good")
- **One new Moon/Mercury (impact-airless) preset** to judge the increment on; Frozen
  gets the crater overprint through the same layer, answering the "bumpy" note. ("Yes, sounds good")
- Crater statistics at the **rubric's basis-level bar**: a power-law size mix whose
  count/size shift with gravity and age — no gold-plating. ("Sounds good")
- Craters ≠ volcanoes read-collision: **"The craters are probably not the problem
  here, it's the volcanoes — they need to be more visually legible as volcanoes."**
  Volcano legibility stays ROUTED to the V2-7/V2-8 expression increments (planned in
  the roadmap, not yet scoped as contracts) — it is NOT a V2-5 burden.
- Atmospheric shielding **deferred** ("Defer is fine").
- **MULTIPLY discipline confirmed** ("Yes"): density/size are continuous functions of
  gravity + age, same pattern as the four prior MULTIPLY increments.
