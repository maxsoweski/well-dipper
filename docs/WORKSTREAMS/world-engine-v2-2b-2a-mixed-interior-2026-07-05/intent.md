# world-engine-v2-2b-2a-mixed-interior — intent

## Why we care

The whole condition-first re-founding exists to answer one felt complaint (Max, verbatim, ROADMAP §0):
*"every stagnant-lid world is a re-rolled Venus"* — he wants condition **combinations** to produce
**predicted-but-never-observed** landforms, not re-rolls of the catalog.

Everything so far has been setup. V2-2a built the router; V2-2b-1 made the *stagnant end* respond to
its drivers (the between-world "re-rolled Venus" fix). **This increment is where the engine first
generates a world that is on no archetype menu** — the `mixed` interior of the response space, where
strong centers *pierce* into shields while weaker ones *tent* into coronae on the same body. That is
the payoff the re-founding was for.

**2b-2a's specific job:** prove the novel mixed primitive is *real and legible* on a **known-answer
world (Tharsis)** — objectively, with numbers an agent can check — **before** 2b-2b bets the pilot's
believability on the honesty-critical seeded-middle worlds (wet-stagnant, corona-pierced). This is
R-phantom's highest-risk item: the program's first genuinely novel generative primitive (every prior
increment cloned `plates.js`). De-risk the machinery on the easy end first; that's why we split here.

*(Captured from Max's signed-off ROADMAP-v2 §0/§5 + this session's scope choices — Max edits if the
framing is off.)*

## Success criteria (Max's language)

- When I dial the mixed sliders to a **Tharsis-like** setting (strong lid, low vigor, few centers), I
  see **1–3 big volcanoes sitting on old flat ground with a rift** — discrete piles, not a blurred
  average of "some volcano everywhere."
- **Every spot on the world is clearly ONE kind of landform** — a shield *or* plains *or* rift — never
  a smeared 50/50 blend. The boundaries between them are crisp.
- The tall volcanoes **never poke up so high they erase the tessera → plains → rift pecking order**
  (no landform can climb over another's floor).
- **Venus, Lava, and Magma still render byte-for-byte identical** — building the mixed interior broke
  nothing that already ships.
- There's **a number** (the interpenetration statistic) built and checked out on Tharsis, ready to
  later tell a genuinely-new mixed world apart from "two catalog worlds glued side by side" — that's
  the test 2b-2b will lean on.

## Explicitly NOT this increment (→ 2b-2b)

The pilot **UAT** (does a mixed world read coherent / distinct / never-observed — Max's taste gate);
the **wet-stagnant** world + its `effectiveL` seeded-pick hand-up; the **corona-pierced** compound
landform + the falsification-grade "pierce and tent *interpenetrate*, not tile" claim. 2b-2a builds
the machinery + the instrument and validates them on the derivable end (Tharsis); 2b-2b uses them to
falsify the mush risk on the hard worlds.
