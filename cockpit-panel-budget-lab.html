What a cockpit readout can say at the game's resolution

measuring…

“I want the whole game to read as a 5th gen game … some things like the resolution are harder limits to get that aesthetic; so we simply need to redesign anything that does not read properly at this new resolution.” — Max, 2026-09-06

1 · INFO — the panel the question was asked about

INFO sits on Screen_LL, the lower and wider pair, so its values get five characters. Left is what today's mechanism puts on the glass at that size; middle is the recommendation; right is what keeping the full strings would actually cost.

2 · DRIVE — with CAP and TURN cut

DRIVE is on Screen_UR, the upper pair: four characters to a value, and eight across the whole panel. That is why the mass-lock banner has to change too — its shipped text is TOO CLOSE — SUBLIGHT ONLY, twenty-five characters.

3 · TARGET — the short name and the distance

TARGET shares the lower pair with INFO, so it also gets nine characters. It is worth seeing what today's column is: that panel is already broken, before any of this work — it prints the full designation and, when that overflows, shrinks it straight past the point a letter is a letter.

4 · The glyph ladder — every character this face can draw

Shown at the size a panel actually draws it, so the judgement about the face is made on the same ink the panels use.
