CHANGES
1. REAR VIEW: add a rear view to the sheet. Views are now side, front, REAR and top, orthographic, same scale, on one shared baseline, plus a small three-quarter view and the small black silhouette.
2. THRUSTERS FACE BACKWARD: the engine nozzles are recessed into sockets in the ship's REAR face and point straight back (along the ship's length). In the side view they are not seen face-on; in the rear view you look straight into them.
3. HANGING COCKPIT: the glass bubble cockpit hangs below the front of the ship, slung underneath the forward hull like a gondola or a helicopter's chin bubble: the main hull sits above and behind it, and the bubble's bottom is the lowest point of the ship. The pilot inside sees forward, sideways and down. Keep its two thick frame pillars and BUBBLE 2.6 m.
4. MORE FOSS: Images 2-4 are Chris Foss paintings. Take more from them: heavy, industrial, slightly asymmetric masses, chunky add-on modules, the sense of a big working machine. Still a small ship (18 m) with few, large features; nothing smaller than 30 cm; no fine panel lines, rivets or rows of tiny windows.
5. COLOUR = PART MAP, NOT PAINT: drop the stripe/band paint scheme entirely. Colour each PART of the ship one flat colour so the sheet shows which part is which, and add a small legend (colour swatch + part name) on the sheet. Use these:
   - BUBBLE (glass): pale blue
   - COCKPIT FRAME (the gondola holding the bubble, and its pillars): cream
   - SPINE: orange
   - DECKS (the stacked hull rolls): yellow
   - UNDERSLUNG MODULES: teal
   - ENGINE BLOCK (the rear section holding the nozzles): mid grey
   - NOZZLES (inside the sockets): near-black
   - HATCH: red
   If you add a part not on this list, give it a new distinct flat colour and add it to the legend.

KEEP: orthographic views on one baseline at one scale (now side, front, rear, top); small three-quarter view; small black silhouette; the 1.8 m human figure beside the side view; the labels "LENGTH 18 m" and "BUBBLE 2.6 m" and the part names BUBBLE, SPINE, DECKS, ENGINES, HATCH; the ship about ten figure-heights long; flat colour fills with dark outlines on a plain off-white background; no numbers, insignia, flames or exhaust, no scene, no cast shadows. Add nothing not listed above.

Tolerance: proportions are judged by eye and by the labels; a drawing that runs 20 % long or short against the figure is not a failure.
